💌 A Letter For Leonando

Happy 18th Birthday, Leonando 🤍

Happy birthday to someone who means so much to me.

I honestly don’t know where to start because there are so many things I want to say to you. But if there’s one thing I want you to know on your 18th birthday, it’s that I’m genuinely grateful that I got to know you and be a part of your life.

Turning 18 is such a special milestone. You’re entering a new chapter of your life, and I hope this chapter brings you so many beautiful things. I hope you get closer to your dreams, meet people who truly appreciate you, and experience countless moments that make you genuinely happy.

I know life won’t always be easy. There will be days when you feel tired, lost, or unsure of yourself. When those days come, I hope you remember that you don’t always have to be strong on your own. You’re allowed to rest, to feel, to make mistakes, and to take your time. And if you ever need someone to listen, I hope you know that I’ll always be here for you.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for every little thing you do, every laugh we share, every conversation we have, and every memory we’ve made together. Some of those moments might seem small or ordinary, but they mean more to me than you probably realize.

I don’t know exactly what the future holds for us, and I don’t want to make promises about things we can’t control. But I do know that, right now, I’m incredibly happy that I get to share a part of this journey with you.

I hope you’ll always be proud of the person you’re becoming. I hope you never forget how worthy you are of love, happiness, and all the good things life has to offer.

And, selfishly, I hope that among all the beautiful things waiting for you in this new chapter, I’ll still be there beside you.

So, happy 18th birthday, Leonando.

I hope 18 brings you more happiness, love, and beautiful memories than you could ever imagine.

Thank you for existing, for being exactly who you are, and for letting me love you.

I love you, always. ❤️

— Yilaa 🖤
